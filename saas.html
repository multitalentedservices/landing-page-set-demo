Features that earn their place

Fast invoicing

Create and send a branded invoice in under two minutes, from phone or laptop.

Automatic reminders

Polite payment reminders go out on your schedule — email first, WhatsApp on Growth and Pro.

Inventory basics

Track stock counts with low-stock flags, tied to the items on your invoices.

Payments recorded

Cash, bank transfer and card payments recorded against each invoice, with a clear balance.

Simple reports

Monthly sales, outstanding amounts and top customers — one screen, no spreadsheet wrestling.

Multi-user ready

Owner, accountant and staff roles with sensible permissions on Growth and Pro plans.

How it works

1
Add your business. Name, logo and tax details once — they appear on every invoice.
2
Send invoices. Pick a customer, add items, send by email or WhatsApp.
3
Get paid, automatically chased. Reminders follow up; you record the payment when it lands.
