How a project runs

1
Listen. A short call and a shorter brief — what must change, for whom, by when.
2
Set the words. The line and the type system come first; everything else follows them.
3
Make it move. Design, motion and build in one pass, reviewed on a real phone.
4
Hand over the system. Files, guidelines and templates your team can actually run.
