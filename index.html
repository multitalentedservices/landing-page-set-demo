How the demo wiring works

Lead form → Sheet log

Every form appends a row (timestamp, page, name, phone, interest) to a local sheet log in your browser. In production this posts to the client's Google Sheet — the note on each form says so honestly.

GA4 + Pixel events (local stubs)

Analytics config holds DEMO placeholder IDs only. A local track() function records page_view, lead_submit and whatsapp_click events to a local event log. No live analytics endpoint is called anywhere.

Verify it yourself

Submit a lead or tap WhatsApp on any page, then open the Demo Logs page — your rows and events appear there in tables, with a clear-logs control.

DEMO labelling: Billora, Studio Kinetiq and CoolCare are fictional. No real clients, testimonials or results are claimed. Demo data lives only in your browser's local storage.
